Big Data and Storage: What are the Trends?

As cloud computing is constantly disrupting traditional business models and large data keeps growing at tremendous rates, techies and traders alike are searching for top trends which will change the way we conduct business in 2016 and beyond.   1. Hybrid and public clouds
  2. Machine learning
  3. Advancing automotive technology
  4. Emotion recognition software
