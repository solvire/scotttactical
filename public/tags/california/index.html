    Mono Lake, California

    Mono Lake



    Mono Lake Tufa State Reserve A large, shallow saline soda lake in Mono County, next to the city of Lee Vining, California

    Mono Lake Tufa State Reserve

    This lake was formed at least 760,000 years ago as a terminal lake in an endorheic basin. This means minerals flowed in from underground rivers and then sprung up through the ground to create those amazing Tufas you see marking the shores.

    Yosemite National Park, CA - October 9, 2015

    Hiking Yosemite, CA

    Rimrise and Shine

    Mornings in Yosemite Valley are breathtaking. The weather was perfect IMO.

    We had some unfortunate luck with the camping and as you can see we could not get duplicate nights on our spot. See our note on getting your site well in advance.

    Our wonderful neighbors were kind enough to help us out. They got us from the upper pines campsites out around the valley and back to the trailhead of the 4 mile trail.
